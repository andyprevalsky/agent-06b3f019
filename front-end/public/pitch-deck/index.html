Current AI Systems Have Hit a Fundamental Scaling Limit
Monolithic and unable to parallelize intelligence.
• Monolithic models function as single, indivisible units of compute.
• Pre & post training returns are starting to diminish.
• Super-Intelligence is not a model that can one-shot the perfect answer in a vacuum without real-world feedback. Super-Intelligence is a system that can explore its environment with mathematical precision.
• This is a fundamental architectural constraint, not just a compute problem.
Mass Agent Networks Are the Evolutionary Leap to Multicellular AI
Minimizes costs while maximizing parallelizeability.
• Divide complex tasks into small, tractable components
• Run thousands of reasoning paths in parallel
• Cross-verify work to eliminate errors and drift
• Self-organize into higher-order structures
