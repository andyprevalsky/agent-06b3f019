# Rose Labs — One-Pager (v0.4)

**Thousands of agents, one finished job.** Rose runs thousands of AI agents on one real operational job (collecting signatures, chasing documents, onboarding merchants) and keeps the record of what worked.

**Thesis:** intelligence is getting cheap; knowing how the real world responds is not. That knowledge only exists once someone does the work, and every task we run adds to a shared, perishable map of merchants, portals and approval chains that makes the next task faster and cheaper for every customer.

**Proof:** Rappi pilot agreed (20 seats, starting soon, no signed contract yet) to coordinate signatures from 100,000+ restaurants in Mexico for a launch six months behind schedule. 5 Fortune 500s in pipeline. Task completion ~25% today, ~$2 per typical task.

**Market:** operations work for Latin American marketplaces and fintechs, paid from headcount and outsourcing budgets. Bounded tasks now, end-to-end workflows next, whole functions after.
