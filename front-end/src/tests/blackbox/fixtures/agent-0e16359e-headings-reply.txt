## Important ratio correction
The verified Giranda label is **per 2 tablets**: **1,200 mg myo-inositol + 300 mg D-chiro-inositol + 400 mcg Quatrefolic® (5-MTHF)**. Those quantities equal **4:1**, not 40:1. A true 40:1 formula containing 1,200 mg myo-inositol would contain only 30 mg D-chiro-inositol. Therefore, products marketed as 40:1 are not exact quantitative substitutes for Giranda.
